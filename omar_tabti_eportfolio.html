Research projects (TIPE)

Two modelling and optimisation projects, both built around a numerical model and a measurable objective.

Stirling engine

2025 to 2026
Problem
Find which settings of a Stirling engine give the best thermal efficiency compared with the Carnot limit.
Method
Modelled the thermodynamic cycle and compared several numerical integration methods on accuracy to pick the most reliable solver.
Outcome
Optimised the engine parameters in Python to maximise efficiency relative to Carnot.

Flettner rotor

2024 to 2025
Problem
Size a Flettner rotor, a wind-assisted propulsion device based on the Magnus effect, for the best aerodynamic efficiency.
Method
Built a numerical performance model of the rotor.
Outcome
Optimised its geometry to identify the most efficient configuration.
